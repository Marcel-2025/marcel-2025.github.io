Privacy Policy – DiskAtlas

Effective: 21 November 2026 · Applies to DiskAtlas for Android and Windows · Controller: Flondy Dev. (Marcel Single) · Contact: flondy.dev@gmail.com

In short: DiskAtlas processes all data locally on your device only. There is no account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking and no transfer of your files or results to us or to third parties.

1. What data does the app process?

  • Files and disk images you choose: DiskAtlas only reads them to find files inside. The source is never changed.
  • Folders you share (Android) or select (Windows): file names, sizes and modification dates are read to build file lists.
  • Sessions, lists, settings, export receipts and reports: stored only in app storage or in folders you choose.
  • Windows drives: with administrator rights and at your request, DiskAtlas reads drives directly and health values (S.M.A.R.T.). This data also stays on your PC.

2. What the app does not do

  • No transfer of files, file names or results to servers
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No location, contact or account data

3. Purchases

Purchases (e.g. “DiskAtlas Pro” or design collections) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Microsoft Store. We receive no payment data. The Google Privacy Policy or the Microsoft Privacy Statement applies.

4. Permissions

  • File picker / folder access (Android): only for files and folders you explicitly choose
  • Billing (Google Play): for in-app purchases
  • Administrator rights (Windows, optional): for direct drive access

5. Diagnostics

A diagnostics file is only created when you export it yourself. Personal paths are hidden by default. It is never sent automatically.

6. Storage and deletion

Sessions can be removed in the app. Uninstalling deletes the app data. Exported files and reports stay in folders you choose until you delete them.

7. Your rights

As we do not store personal data about you, we cannot provide information about data we do not have. Questions: flondy.dev@gmail.com.
