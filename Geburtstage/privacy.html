Privacy policy for Geburtstage

18 November 2026 · Controller: Flondy Dev. (Marcel Single) · flondy.dev@gmail.com

Your entries are stored locally in the app’s private storage. The app does not require an account and has no cloud service or advertising.

1. Data processed by the app

  • Entries you create or import include names, dates of birth, groups, notes, gift ideas, budgets and events.
  • With permission, contact import reads names, birthdays and contact photo references and saves them locally.
  • With permission, calendar sync writes events to a calendar on your device.
  • Optional Facebook import displays Facebook in an embedded browser. You sign in directly with Facebook. The app does not see or save your password. It reads birthday information visible to you on Facebook’s birthday page, including name, date and profile link. See Meta’s privacy policy.
  • Reminder notifications are generated on your device.

2. Advertising and analytics

The app does not send your entries to Flondy Dev. or third parties. It uses no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs or location data.

3. In-app purchases

Google Play processes Plus and tip payments. Flondy Dev. does not receive payment details. See Google’s privacy policy.

4. Permissions

Contact access is optional for import. Calendar read/write access is optional for sync. Notifications enable reminders. Internet access is used to display Facebook during import.

5. Storage and deletion

You can delete individual entries or all entries in the app. Uninstalling removes its private data. You choose where to save exported JSON backups or ICS files.

6. Your rights

Flondy Dev. does not hold copies of your personal entries and cannot provide access to data it does not hold. For questions, email flondy.dev@gmail.com.
