Import birthdays

Bring in dates from contacts, an ICS file or the birthday page you see when signed in to Facebook. The app checks for duplicates.

Make it personal

Keep gift ideas and spending next to the person. Greeting templates fill in their name and age. Share the finished card as a picture.

Get a timely reminder

Choose the birthday itself, the day before or a week ahead. Two home screen widgets show upcoming birthdays or a countdown.

Keep your calendar close

Manage entries locally without ads. Export yearly events as ICS or back up your data as JSON. Facebook import and Google Play purchases require a connection.
