Local progress and input

Hexora stores your progress (lessons, coins, settings, chosen themes) only on your device. The app has no user account, no ads and no analytics services. Anything you enter into the tools is processed on the device only and is not transmitted.

Google Play purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes the data required for this under its own privacy policy. The app only learns which products are unlocked.

Backups and deletion

If Android's device backup is switched on, your progress may be part of that backup. You delete all data by choosing “Reset progress” in the app or by uninstalling it.
