Privacy policy

For the Riff music learning app and this website.

Controller and contact

Marcel Single, Flondy Dev., is responsible for Riff and the content of this website. Contact him at flondy.dev@gmail.com. Provider details in the legal notice.

Microphone, USB audio and MIDI

Riff analyzes audio and MIDI input on your device to assess pitch and timing. During ordinary practice, audio buffers are discarded after analysis. The app does not send your performance to Flondy Dev. You can revoke microphone access in system settings.

Optional recordings and exports

Starting a recording or enabling automatic recording saves your session locally as a WAV file. Play or delete recordings in your profile. Exported song files and CSV statistics remain until you delete them.

Local profile and progress

Preferences, player name, avatar, practice times, scores, badges, setlists and songs are stored in private Android app storage or in %APPDATA%\FlondyRiff on Windows. The portable edition uses its data folder. Clear app data in Android settings or delete the Windows data folder to remove them. Delete external exports separately. The app uses no player accounts, ads or analytics SDKs.

Purchases and license keys

Google Play processes account and payment information for Android purchases. Riff uses purchase entitlements to unlock or restore content. Windows licenses are verified locally and can contain the product, customer name or purchase email. Google Privacy Policy.

Visiting the website

The hosting platform receives connection data such as IP address, requested page and browser information to deliver and secure the website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). This page serves images locally, uses system fonts and sets no tracking cookies of its own. The platform’s privacy policy applies when signing in to the private preview. OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Inquiries and rights

When you email us, we process your address and message to respond. For contractual inquiries the basis is GDPR Article 6(1)(b), otherwise our legitimate interest in responding under Article 6(1)(f). We delete correspondence once resolved and any retention obligations end. Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests and complain to a data protection authority.
