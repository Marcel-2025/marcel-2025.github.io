Getting started.

How do I connect my instrument?

Use your microphone for acoustic guitar or ukulele, a USB audio interface for electric guitar or bass, or USB or Bluetooth MIDI for a keyboard.

How do I practice a tricky passage?

Loop a section at 25–125% speed with Riff Repeater. In Wait Mode, the song pauses until you play the right note.

What can I use for free?

Three songs, the first three lessons of each course, one minigame, Jam Band, tuner, metronome, chords and scales. Full access is designed as a one-time purchase or an optional Riff Plus subscription.

Which devices is Riff designed for?

Riff 1.2.0 is in development for Windows and Android, with portrait and landscape layouts for phones and tablets. The app views shown here were captured from the desktop build.
