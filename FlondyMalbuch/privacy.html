Privacy policy

App “Flondy Malbuch” (Android, package dev.flondy.malbuch) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: The app does not collect personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking. Your pictures stay on your device.

1. Controller

Flondy Dev., email: flondy.dev@gmail.com

2. Data on your device

Coloring progress, finished pictures, settings (design, language, animations) and achievements are stored only locally in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or third parties and are deleted when you uninstall the app. When you share a picture or save it to your gallery, you decide where it goes.

3. Purchases via Google Play

In-app purchases (Premium, packs, thank-you coffee) are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment data is handled only by Google; we receive no payment or account data. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Links to Amazon and other sites

The app contains links to our books on Amazon and to YouTube and Facebook. Only when you tap a link does the page open in your browser or the respective app; the provider's privacy policy applies there.

5. Permissions

The app only needs the permission for Google Play purchases. Pictures are saved to “Pictures/Flondy Malbuch” using Android's save function, without access to your other photos.

6. Children

The app collects no data from children either. Purchases are protected by Google Play settings (e.g. password prompt, Family Link).

7. Your rights

As we store no personal data, we cannot provide information about stored data. For questions, write to flondy.dev@gmail.com.
